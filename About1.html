About Blood Donation

Why Donate Blood ?


❤️ Blood donation in a selfless act of kindness

that can make a huge difference in someone's life.

❤️ Every two seconds, someone in the world

needs blood.

❤️ Your donation can help save a life, support

medical treatments and emergencies.

Benefits of Blood Donation



Good for Heart


Reduce risk of heart diseases.



Health Checkup


Free mini health checkup every time.



Saves Lives


Your blood can save multiple lives.



New Blood Cells


Stimulates new blood cell production.

Eligibility Criteria


✅ Age between 18 to 65 years


✅ Weight must be 50kg or more


✅ Homoglobin level should be normal


✅ Body temperature should be normal


✅ Generally in good health

Myths vs Facts


❤️ Myth:Donating blood weakness the body.

Fact:It does not,your body recovers quickly.


❤️ Myth:Only rare blood groups are needed.

Fact:All blood groups are important.


❤️ Myth:Blood donation is painful.

Fact:It is completely safe and only takes 10-15 minute.
